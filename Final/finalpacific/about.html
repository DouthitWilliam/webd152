Meet the Team

Our President

President of the Pacific Trails Resort, Joe Newsprout stems from right here in California, growing up fascinated by nature. Since the past 10 years Newsprout has shown to be a great leader for the resort, putting a heavy emphasis on the enjoyment of the guests and has been shown to be more than capable handling things at the resort. Newsprout guides his employees with his positive attitude and loves to welcome all guests with a smile.

"Growing up I've always appreciated the beauty of this place. Hiking, kayaking, even watching the sunset with my folks, they're all core memories to me. I think others should experience these memories too."

Trail Leaders

If you wish to go on a guided hike, one of our many trained trail leaders will gladly accompany you. No matter what pace you hike at or what your experience level is with hiking, our trail leaders will make sure to never leave you behind. Our trail leaders will also happily share information about the local flora, fauna, and geological facts along your hike.

Housekeepers

Worried about returning to a dirty yurt? Worry not! Our housekeepers will keep your yurt tidy while you're out enjoying the sights.

Our Values

Exploration

We believe everyone has an adventurous spirit in them. With our hiking trails and ocean kayaks we would like to bring that spirit out.

Relaxation

Everyone needs a good getaway once in a while, if you're not feeling adventurous we encourage you to lounge in our outdoor pool or try some birdwatching.

Making Memories

We want to make sure a trip to Pacific Trails Resort is one you will never forget. Book your trip today and immerse yourself with all there is to see.
